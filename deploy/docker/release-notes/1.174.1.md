## 1.174.1 (2026-10-30)

### Bug Fixes

* **audit:** **Details** opens and closes again on the **Audit Log**. Since 1.171.1 a click left the row empty and the button never changed to *Hide*
* **backups:** dropdowns on **Fleet tasks → Backup** stay open. The **time picker**, **When to back up** and **Destination** closed by themselves every few seconds while the hub considered a backup to be running, because the page redrew the whole schedule card each time it checked on the run. It now redraws only the list of recent backups and the *Next due* line
* **backups:** changing the schedule no longer rebuilds the picker under you a second later; the tab waits until you have left the field
* **console:** on every page, a dropdown or date/time field you are using is no longer closed by a live update. The update is applied as soon as you leave the field, or after 30 seconds without a click or key press on the page

### Notes

* With a column filter on the Audit Log, an open **Details** row now hides together with its line and comes back open when the filter is cleared
* No agent update is needed
