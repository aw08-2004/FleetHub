## 1.164.0 (2026-10-22)

### Features

* **remote:** a new quality preset, **Auto**, is now the default. On a session relayed through the hub it lowers the frame rate and bitrate on its own when the picture falls behind (as far as 6 fps at 0.4 Mbps), and raises them again once the connection keeps up (up to 20 fps at 6 Mbps). The current bitrate is shown next to the picture size. Over a direct connection Auto stays at 15 fps and 2.5 Mbps
* **remote:** the fixed presets give a good connection more room: **Quality** is now 30 fps at 12 Mbps, **Balanced** 20 fps at 6 Mbps and **Speed** 12 fps at 2 Mbps at 75 % scale

### Bug Fixes

* **remote:** a relayed session no longer replays old motion on a slow connection. On a phone over mobile data the picture could keep moving for several seconds after the remote mouse had stopped. A viewer that falls behind now skips straight to the newest picture, holding the image still for a moment instead of showing movement that has already happened

No agent update is needed.
