## 1.149.0 (2026-10-14)

### Features

* **recordings:** carry a recording on as its next part when the PC switches session. Switching user, signing in or signing out relaunches the remote helper, which used to end every recording at the first switch. The viewer now waits for the new picture and starts **Part 2** by itself: same series, same reason, no dialog, its shares copied, and its own badge confirmation before anything is stored. The series keeps its ten-minute deadline (a switch in the last minute leaves up to a minute, never more than one minute past the agreed time), continuing is accepted only for the same operator and session within 2 minutes and up to 20 parts, and sharing any part shares all of them. **Stop recording** never continues (#131) (80fed08) (5accf24) (2e435ba)
* **recordings:** label each row on the Recordings page *Part N* (#131) (80fed08)
